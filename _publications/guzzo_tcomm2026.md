We propose two new precoding algorithms for downlink multi-user massive MIMO systems that take into account and mitigate the effects of the non-linear Power Amplifiers (PA) contained in the antennas. The main objective of the proposed Power Amplifier PrecOding (PAPO) methods
is to ensure the PA operates efficiently while minimizing its saturation effects. The proposed approach is based on a projected gradient descent with a hard or soft PA power input constraint for which we prove convergence. The new gradient expression is derived from the amplitude PA characteristic with respect to the real and imaginary parts entanglement. The proposed precoding methods show a significant improvement in all metrics: Peak to Average Power Ratio (PAPR), Error Vector Magnitude (EVM) and Bit Error Rate (BER) without degrading
the spectral leakage in the adjacent bands measured by the Adjacent Channel Power Ratio (ACPR). Moreover, they maintain the operating point within the PA optimal energy-efficiency range.
