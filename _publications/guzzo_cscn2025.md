**Disclamer :** This paper was accepted as a poster explaining its short length.

We discuss the non dependence of the peak to average power ratio (PAPR) with respect to the number of antennas at the base station (BS) in the case of perfect channel state information (CSI) for downlink multi-user massive MIMO systems despite what was supposed and intuited in the literature. This study was important before introducing a non-linear power amplifier in the chain at the transmitter. Indeed, knowledge of the PAPR behavior and the PA can lead to better sizing of the system so that the PA can be used at its optimum operating point in terms of energy consumption.
