We propose a closed form expression of the Signalto-Distorsion-Noise Ratio (SDNR) for a downlink multi-user massive MIMO system. We consider the influence of the nonlinearity induced by a Rapp Power Amplifier (PA) of the RF chain assuming a perfect Channel State Information (CSI). We set the system power to ensure the PA is efficient and that the Error Vector Magnitude (EVM) is limited.
