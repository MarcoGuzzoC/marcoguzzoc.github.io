We propose a closed form expression of the Signal-to-Distorsion-Noise Ratio (SDNR) for a downlink multi-user massive MIMO system. We consider the influence of the nonlinearity induced by a Rapp Power Amplifier (PA) of the RF chain assuming a perfect Channel State Information (CSI). We set the system power to ensure the PA is efficient and that the Error Vector Magnitude (EVM) is limited.

We found that the Peak to Average Power Ratio (PAPR) is constant with respect to the inverse load \\(\gamma = \frac{M}{K}\\). We have shown that for the Rapp PA, we should use a certain input power \\(p_s \propto \gamma(\gamma−1)\\) to maximize the power efficiency constraint and ensure a limited signal distorsion. Thus we propose a closed-form expression of the SDNR using Bussgang linearization and an asymptotic gaussian approximation. The SDNR shows three areas with respect to \\(p_s\\) : linear regime, intermediate area and saturation zone. The SDNR demonstrating a maximum; our optimization of the input power facilitates the reach of good performance.
