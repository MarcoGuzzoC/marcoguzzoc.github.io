For downlink multi-user massive MIMO systems, we provide an upper bound and an analytical expression of the peak to average power ratio in the case of Rayleigh channel. In contrast to what was assumed and intuited in the literature, we examine a variety of linear precoders and explain how the PAPR is independent of the base station’s antenna count when we have perfect channel state information
