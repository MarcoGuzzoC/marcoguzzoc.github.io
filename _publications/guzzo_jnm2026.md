In the context of a massive MIMO downlink communication system, we propose an analytical expression for the minimum number of antennas required to ensure that a certain level of distortion caused by the power amplifiers at the transmitter is not exceeded.

We demonstrate the influence of an AP in a multi-user MIMO communication chain and propose a method for determining the number of antennas that takes this into account. This bound requires a few easily measurable values, as well as an estimate of the order of magnitude of the average attenuation due to distance and obstruction for each user.
