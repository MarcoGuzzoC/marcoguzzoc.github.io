Study of random processes thanks to its low order characteristics (1-2 orders only): stationarity, filtering but also estimation and modeling as AR(M) processes using Yule-Walker equations.

**Topics:** Process stationarity (1st and 2nd order), impact of filtering on processes properties and characteristics, estimation and estimators reliability (bias, MSE...) and modeling as AR(M) processes using Yule-Walker equations.

**Level:** M1 students

**Teaching load:** 16h
