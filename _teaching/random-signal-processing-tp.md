Study of random processes thanks to its low order characteristics (1-2 orders only): stationarity, filtering but also estimation and modeling as an AR(M) using Yule-Walker equations.

**Topics:** Project on speech processing : the goal of this project is to implement a human voice encoding and decoding system using linear predictive coding. After a study of signal characteristics, a focus is made on applying effects on a generated signal. The process is decomposed in three steps:

- Analysis of speech characteristics.
- Manipulation of these characteristics to achieve a desired effect (for example, a change in frequency).
- Synthesis of speech based on the modified characteristics.

**Level:** M1 students

**Teaching load:** 16h
