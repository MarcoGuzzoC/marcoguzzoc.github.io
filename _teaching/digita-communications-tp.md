Introduction to digital communications: baseband and bandpass communications.

**Topics:**

- First lab: BPSK modulation on AWGN channel with introduction of ISI and comparison to standard BER curves with Monte-Carlo simulations.
- Second lab: 16-QAM modulation on AWGN channel with Gray mapping and comparison to standard BER curves with Monte-Carlo simulations.

**Level:** M1 students

**Teaching load:** 8h
