# About me

Currently PhD student at [ETIS](https://www.etis-lab.fr) where I'm investigating about non-linearities impairments in (Cell-Free) Massive MIMO system. In 2024, I earned my Master’s degree in Computer Science and Complex Systems Engineering, with a specialization in **Signal Processing**, **Information Theory**, and **Telecommunications** from [CY Cergy Paris University](https://www.cyu.fr) while also completing my engineering degree with a specialization in **Signal Processing** and **Artificial Intelligence** from [ENSEA](https://www.ensea.fr).
